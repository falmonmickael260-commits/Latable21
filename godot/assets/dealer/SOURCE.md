# Dealer head

The head in `dealer-head.glb` is derived from the MakeHuman hm08 base mesh,
explicitly released under CC0 in September 2020.

Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
License: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md

Original copyright holders listed by that source: Data Collection AB,
Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier (2020).

The game uses only the head, with fitted hair, eyeballs, irises and eyebrows
added by `scripts/build-dealer-head.py`. The shipped GLB is prebuilt.
Its jacket, hands and articulated arm are assembled by Godot.
